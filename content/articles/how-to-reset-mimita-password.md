## Forgot Your Password?

If you cannot remember your Mimita account password, you can reset it from the website. Here is how.

---

## Step 1: Go to the Sign In Page

Navigate to the [sign in page](/signin) on mimita.fun.

---

## Step 2: Click "Forgot Password"

Below the sign in form, there is a link to reset your password. Click it.

You will be asked to enter the email address associated with your account.

---

## Step 3: Check Your Email

A 6-digit verification code will be sent to your email. This code expires after a short time, so do not wait too long to use it.

If you do not see the email, check your spam folder.

---

## Step 4: Enter the Code

On the website, enter the 6-digit code you received. After verification, you will be able to set a new password.

---

## Step 5: Choose a New Password

Your new password should be:

- At least 8 characters long
- Not the same as your old password
- Something you have not used on other sites

---

## Step 6: Sign In

After the password is changed, you will be redirected to sign in with your new password.

---

## Troubleshooting

### I did not receive the email
Make sure you entered the correct email address. Check your spam folder. If it still does not arrive, the email server may be down — try again later.

### The code expired
Request a new code from the password reset page.

### I do not remember my email
If you do not remember which email you used, contact support through the community channels on the [socials page](/socials).

### The account was deleted
If the account was deleted, signing up again with the same email will create a fresh account.
