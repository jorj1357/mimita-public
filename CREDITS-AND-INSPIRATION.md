## Rocket League / RocketSim reference

Rocket League is a game by Psyonix/Epic Games. MiMITA is not affiliated with
Psyonix, Epic Games, or RocketSim, and does not ship Rocket League code or
assets.

[RocketSim](https://github.com/ZealanL/RocketSim) by ZealanL is an
MIT-licensed reverse-engineered simulation used as a public reference for the
vehicle arena. Pinned reference commit:
`c2baacb8f4b441dd8505e63c2aeb5a1679b60b02` (2026-07-14).

MiMITA references RocketSim's published constants and behavior model: car mass
180, gravity 6.5 m/s^2, car-world friction 0.3 and restitution 0.3, auto-roll
force 1 m/s^2 and torque 80, auto-flip impulse 2 m/s and torque 50 rad/s^2 for
0.4 s, sticky force 0.5-1.5 g, non-sticky friction 0.1/0.5/1.0 by surface
normal, and boost along the car's local forward axis. No RocketSim source is
vendored; MiMITA's solver and implementation are its own.
