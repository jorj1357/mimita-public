4. config!!!!
++ the game is super duper editable!!!!!!
++ ingame settings are like 5% of the things u can edit
++ "mimita-game\config\weapons.json" , u can edit reload times, damage of evry weapon
++ "mimita-game\config\postfx.json" , u can make cool visuals trippy visuals
++ movement.json, switch from default to source, and edit both movement modes to ur liking 
++ gameplay.json , different aim modes are fun to use, default is crosshair, but there is physical and camforward modes too
++ camconfig.json, u can move ur offset from ur character
++ impact_decals.json , switch it to "enabled" at the top instead of "disabled" it might run slower, but has blood and bullet holes/cracks appear in world where u shoot!! it looks cool 
++ npcconfig (?), u can make npcs harder /easier (SRY THAT 1 person who said npcs cooking  my fault)
