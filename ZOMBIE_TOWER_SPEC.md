# MiMITA Zombie Tower: Design Requirements

## Purpose and scope

This document records permanent Zombie Tower project requirements. It does not claim implementation or runtime acceptance. It supplements the gameplay contract in `docs/specs/gamemodes/zombie-tower/zombie-tower.md` and supersedes its earlier tentative 50-floor target where they conflict.

## Tower and traversal

- The finished tower has 100 floors. Each floor occupies approximately 100 m × 100 m; typical perimeter or partition walls are about 50 m high.
- Players progress from the bottom to the top. Every floor has at least one traversable connection to the next, and floor 100 ends in an open-air rooftop arena.
- At least 75% of floors use non-standard layouts. The layout vocabulary includes tunnels, curved and spiral paths, long corridors, lava pits, narrow bridges, obstacles, floating platforms, vertical chambers, broken architecture, and alternate routes.
- Floating platforms may be 5–50 m apart only where the traversal capabilities available to the intended actors make the gap reachable.
- Fast movement, jumping, dashing, projectiles, collision, and NPC navigation must work on the authored geometry. Players and NPCs use shared movement, collision, and navigation owners where practical; NPCs may use only abilities available to their role.
- Geometry and gameplay placement should be generated by native MiMITA map tooling where practical. Blender may supply decoration, but is not required for routine floor production.
- Reusable procedural rules and deterministic seeds must make generated layouts reproducible and editable.

## Encounters and completion

- Boss encounters are on floors 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100.
- Defeating the floor-100 boss wins the run.
- Encounter, checkpoint, spawn, hazard, and boss behavior is authored through the map/entity configuration owners; runtime behavior remains server-authoritative where the mode contract requires it.
- The existing Zombie Tower first-playable contract remains authoritative for phases, team roster, encounters, attempts, checkpoints, results, placeholder boss presentation, and mode presentation unless this document explicitly expands it.

## Delivery and evidence

- Build a connected, playable 10-floor vertical slice first, then scale the same reusable rules toward 100 floors.
- Validate actual player movement and NPC navigation on the slice, including floor transitions, gaps, obstacles, and valid/invalid routes.
- Preserve existing working MiMITA behavior and run relevant regressions after changes.
- Compilation alone is not gameplay proof. Record source, build/executable identity, runtime journal, observed gameplay, and human acceptance separately.

## Does not define

This document does not replace the detailed existing mode specification, define new monster attacks, or claim that the current GLBs are connected or that the movement/NPC acceptance gates have passed.
