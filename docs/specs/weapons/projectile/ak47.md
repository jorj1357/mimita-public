# AK-47

**Purpose:** define the Bloodtest AK-47 as a strong, fully simulated ballistic
rifle.
**Audience:** weapon, projectile, networking, audio, animation, and gamemode
contributors.
**Does not:** define a hitscan fallback, guarantee a final damage number, or
require a particular asset before the weapon contract can be implemented.

## Behavior

- The AK-47 is a strong automatic rifle in the Bloodtest weapon family.
- Every round is a physical projectile with finite travel time, gravity-driven
  bullet drop, and authoritative world/actor collision as specified in
  [projectile firearms](projectile-firearms.md). It never uses hitscan or an
  instant line trace as its damage path.
- Weapon-specific damage, muzzle velocity, gravity scale, drag, spread,
  magazine, reserve, fire cadence, reload, recoil, and equip timings are
  per-weapon JSON values. Damage is resolved from the projectile's configured
  impact profile and actual impact state.
- Recoil moves the held weapon and arms through the shared physical-aim path;
  mouse aim, visual recoil, and the projectile muzzle direction remain
  separately observable and tunable.
- The AK-47 is stronger than the FAMAS. Its relationship to the M4A1 is a
  balance choice; both are strong rifles and need not have identical recoil,
  cadence, or drop.
- World impacts use bullet-hole/debris presentation, never rocket/grenade
  explosion effects. Actor feedback follows Bloodtest's shared blood/body-part
  response.

## Acceptance

Show finite projectile travel and measurable drop at range, server-confirmed
collision and damage, physical recoil, configurable live tuning, and correct
non-explosive impact presentation in a pinned Bloodtest run. Performance must
be reviewed with many actors firing concurrently; build success is not
acceptance.
