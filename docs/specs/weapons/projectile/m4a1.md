# M4A1

**Purpose:** define the M4A1 as the second strong Bloodtest rifle.
**Audience:** weapon, projectile, networking, audio, animation, and gamemode
contributors.
**Does not:** define hitscan behavior or require AK-47-identical handling.

## Behavior

- The M4A1 is a strong automatic rifle, comparable in overall role to the AK-47
  but with independently tunable handling, recoil, cadence, spread, velocity,
  and drop.
- Every round is an individually simulated physical projectile with finite
  travel time and gravity-driven drop. Hitscan and instant-beam damage are
  forbidden. Follow [projectile firearms](projectile-firearms.md) for shared
  authority, prediction, reconciliation, and load rules.
- Per-weapon JSON owns damage/impact profile, ballistics, magazine/reserve,
  cadence, reload, physical recoil, sound/model, and equip/unequip timing.
- Use Bloodtest's bullet-hole/debris world impact and blood/body actor feedback;
  no rocket/grenade explosion effects.

## Acceptance

Prove distinct live-tunable behavior from the AK-47, visible projectile drop,
server-confirmed hits, physical recoil, and stable concurrent-fire performance
in a pinned Bloodtest run.
