However, dev-loop accepted the queued `agent.move` request without any corresponding agent-command consumption/completion event. Therefore a controlled, command-driven walk-cycle comparison and visual acceptance were not established. This change addresses the model-axis/bind-frame mismatch; do not claim the walking swing or fine aim-angle gap has been fully accepted until tested in a client that consumes the movement command.
