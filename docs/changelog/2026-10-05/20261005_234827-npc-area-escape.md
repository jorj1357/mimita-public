# NPC local-area trap escape (maximize net movement)

Date (UTC): 2026-10-05
EST timestamp: 2026-10-05 19:48:27 EDT
Branch: `afad20a-rebuild`

## Result

`PASS_WITH_HUMAN_REVIEW`

Human report: NPCs pace in one spot (walk into the wall, step back, walk into the
same wall) with no net movement, and on slopes go halfway up, turn back, turn
around, get stuck, go back down, repeat. Request: track time spent in one area
and, past a limit, do something drastic to get away, maximizing net movement.

## Cause

Nothing measured "am I stuck in the same small area for too long". The local
wall-avoid / backtrack / stuck layers could each keep the actor within a few
metres indefinitely (oscillating), while the route planner kept re-deciding.

## Fix — local-area trap detector + committed breakout

`NpcNavigator` now tracks an anchor position and the time spent inside
`areaEscapeRadiusMeters` of it **while actively trying to move**. When that
exceeds `areaEscapeSeconds`, it commits to a single breakout direction for
`areaEscapeHoldSeconds`:

- The direction maximizes open distance and distance from the anchor, and
  secondarily heads toward the goal (`score = open + 6*dot(away) + 3*dot(goal)`).
- While active it clears the local correction and backtrack and is forced as the
  steering direction (unless a visible enemy owns movement), so wall-avoid /
  backtrack cannot undo it.
- Recovery jumps are suppressed during the breakout; the low-obstacle hop still
  applies.
- A deliberately stopped actor (input ~0, e.g. arrived at its objective) never
  accumulates the timer, so holding position is not misread as a trap.

`src/npc/npc.cpp` calls `updateAreaEscape` after the commitment evaluation and
forces `moveDir` to the breakout direction before `buildInputState`.

### Live knobs (`config/npc-difficulty.json`, hot-reloaded)
`areaEscapeRadiusMeters` (5), `areaEscapeSeconds` (4), `areaEscapeHoldSeconds`
(2). The movement regression selftests pin deterministic values so they guard
the algorithm, not the human's live edits.
